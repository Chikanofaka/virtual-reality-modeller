# Adaptive onboarding round

Current pack: {{planning_pack}}. Evidence: {{evidence_inventory}}. Answered questions: {{answers}}. Blockers: {{gap_list}}.

Choose no more than two high-impact questions for this round. Prefer boundary/scale/entrance/portals/circulation before furniture appearance. State one sentence explaining why each answer matters. Offer two or three clear options when useful, and accept free text or a marked image. Never repeat an answered question without a specific contradiction.

For every answer update its source, confidence, assumptions and resolved/blocking status. Separate owner acceptance of an approximate scale from a measured scale. If source conflicts remain, request a decision rather than deciding silently. If only optional details remain, propose explicit defaults for review.

Output: mission name, two questions, learned facts, remaining blockers and the next unlock. Store CLI summaries under survey, entrance, rooms, furniture, navigation and experience; detailed source references and geometry belong in the planning pack and review.
