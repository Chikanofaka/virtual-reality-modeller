# Provenance and redistribution

The new harness code and documentation use the root MIT license.

The Midtown apartment showcase contains locally authored geometry, procedural
materials, a Blender source/generator and output images under the root MIT license.
One unscaled plan and six private photo observations informed the commission;
the original image files and private approval/conversation records are not included.
Dimensions are accepted estimates and the city backdrop is abstract. A public
reference plan does not confer approval on a new user's design. Historical runtime
evidence and the sanitized public replay are identified separately in the case study.

Three.js 0.180.0 is vendored unchanged from the official `mrdoob/three.js` r180
tag. Its MIT license is in `runtime/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`. Vendoring preserves
the original V7 dependency version and removes the CDN startup requirement.

Pearl Office source code, the floor-plan images, and historical engineering notes
were supplied by the commissioning user in eleven ZIP archives for reuse and
preparation of a public repository. Original archives contain no explicit license
grant. `examples/pearl-office/upstream-v7/` preserves selected source files;
`docs/version-evolution.md` and the evidence manifests identify the source hashes.
The maintained Pearl scene is a derivative of that supplied V7 scene. The root MIT
license must not be interpreted as proof of third-party ownership or a license
grant over historical inputs. Before publishing these supplied assets, the owner
should state their license or remove/replace them. No brand affiliation is claimed.

The repository does not include the private conversation transcript, user account
identifiers, credentials, original local download paths, or unrelated attachments.
Historical notes are evidence of prior statements, not current verification.
